Privacy Policy for Ai Voice Lock Screen
Application Name: Ai Voice Lock Screen
Package Name: com.voice.lock.screen.ai
Effective Date: October 29, 2026
1. Introduction
Ai Voice Lock Screen ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use the Ai Voice Lock Screen mobile application.
Core Privacy Guarantee:
All voice recordings, passcodes, pattern locks, and biometric validations are processed strictly locally on your device. We NEVER record, upload, store, or transmit your voice or security credentials to remote servers.
2. Information Processed Locally on Your Device
- Audio and Voice Data: When you use the voice unlock feature, your microphone captures audio strictly while you are speaking into the unlock prompt. This audio is converted to text in real time using the device's native speech recognition. Audio recordings are discarded immediately after recognition and are never stored or transmitted to external servers.
- Security Credentials: Backup passcodes, PINs, pattern locks, and voice phrases are stored encrypted solely on your local device.
- Biometric Data (Fingerprint): Fingerprint authentication is managed entirely by Android's BiometricPrompt hardware security module. The application never accesses, collects, or stores raw biometric or fingerprint templates.
- Wallpapers: Custom photos or wallpapers selected from your gallery remain strictly on your local device storage.
3. Android Permissions and Why They Are Needed
- Record Audio (RECORD_AUDIO): Required for real-time voice unlock recognition. Audio is not saved or transmitted.
- Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Required to show the lock screen overlay securely over other apps and when waking the device.
- Biometric (USE_BIOMETRIC): Required to enable unlocking with your device's built-in fingerprint sensor.
- Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used exclusively by the App Locker feature to detect when protected apps are opened.
- Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): Required to keep lock screen protection running continuously in the background.
- Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Displays the system-required foreground service notification to ensure continuous protection.
- Run at Startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Restores lock screen protection automatically upon device reboot.
- Ignore Battery Optimizations (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS): Prevents device battery savers from terminating lock screen security unexpectedly.

5. User Choices and Privacy Rights (GDPR and CCPA)
- European Union (GDPR): In the EEA and UK, consent is gathered via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. You can review or modify your consent settings anytime within the App settings.
- California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell personal information. You can opt out of personalized ads by resetting or deleting your Google Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID).
6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)
The application is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that personal data has been provided by a child, we immediately remove it.
7. Data Security and Deletion
All security configurations (voice phrases, PIN codes, patterns, and lock states) are stored locally in private application sandboxes protected by Android OS security. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the application completely deletes all saved credentials and settings from your device.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
